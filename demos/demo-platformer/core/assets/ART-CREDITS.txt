Demo art (I-170)

The character, enemy, trap, checkpoint, terrain and background images in gfx/frog, gfx/pig, gfx/level,
maps/terrain.png and maps/background.png are from the "Pixel Adventure" asset pack by Pixel Frog
(https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/pixel-adventure-1), copied unchanged except for file names from the
developer's copy of the pack. Use follows the licence on the pack's page, which the developer confirmed
allows including them in this project.

The sound effects in sfx/ and the music in music/ are original, generated by
assets-src/demo-platformer/sound/make_sounds.py (I-210).

The font in fonts/ is Press Start 2P (SIL Open Font License, see fonts/OFL.txt).
