Gold in Shadows - credits

Game design, levels and art
  Gold in Shadows is the developer's own game (originally a Godot 4 prototype, then a libGDX game,
  D:\Coding\LibGDX\goldinshadows\gold). The developer created the art and owns it, under their own
  licence; it is used in this Ember2D demo with their permission (2026-11-28).

  Copied unchanged except for file names (original name -> here):
    Character-Sheet.png  -> gfx/character.png
    Enemy-Sheet.png      -> gfx/enemies.png
    TileSet-Sheet.png    -> gfx/tileset.png
    Weapons.png          -> gfx/weapons.png
    Muzzle.png           -> gfx/muzzle.png
    impact-bullet.png    -> gfx/impact.png
    particle.png         -> gfx/particle.png
    StartScreen.png      -> gfx/start-screen.png
    fonts/pixel.fnt, fonts/pixel.png (the original's generated 5x7 pixel font with icon glyphs)

  The maps in maps/ were converted from the original's levels (levels/*.level) by
  assets-src/demo-gold-in-shadows/ConvertLevels.java, which runs the original's own level compiler.

Sound and music
  The original has no audio. The sound effects in sfx/ and the music in music/ are original,
  synthesised by assets-src/demo-gold-in-shadows/sound/make_sounds.py for this demo.
